# Privacy Policy for Pomplings

**Effective date:** October 31, 2026

This privacy policy explains how **Pomplings: Cute Merge Puzzle** ("Pomplings", "the game", "we", "us") handles your information. Pomplings is made by **Bosan TecHub**.

The short version: **Pomplings does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.** The game works fully offline, and everything you do in it stays on your own device.

## Information we collect

We do not collect any information from you. Pomplings:

- Does **not** ask you to create an account or sign in.
- Does **not** ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.
- Does **not** connect to the internet. It sends nothing to us or anyone else.
- Does **not** include advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
- Does **not** use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library.
- Does **not** request any device permissions on Android or iOS.

## Information stored on your device

To let you keep playing where you left off, Pomplings saves a small amount of game data **only on your device**:

- Your game progress: scores, records, friends you have found, goals, daily gift streak, and the buttons and stars you have earned.
- Your settings: sound, music, vibration, number badges and landing guide.
- Your saved basket, so you can continue a game later.
- Basket photos you choose to save from the results screen. These are kept in the game's own private storage, not in your photo gallery.

This data never leaves your device, and we cannot see or access it. You can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling the game or by clearing the app's data in your device settings. If you uninstall the game, your progress cannot be recovered.

Your device's own backup service (such as iCloud or Google backup) may include this game data in your personal backups, depending on your device settings. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, not by us.

## In-app purchases

Pomplings has **no in-app purchases**. The buttons and stars in the game are earned by playing and have no real-world value.

## Children's privacy

Pomplings is suitable for players of all ages. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). This meets the requirements of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR's rules on children's data.

## Third-party services

Pomplings does not use any third-party services that collect data. The game is downloaded through the Apple App Store or Google Play, which have their own privacy policies covering your store account and downloads:

- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. Data protection laws such as the GDPR (European Union and United Kingdom) and the CCPA/CPRA (California) give you rights over personal data a company holds about you; for Pomplings, all game data is on your device and fully under your control.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Pomplings changes how it handles information, we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top. If a change means the game starts collecting any data, we will say so clearly in the app's store listing and in this policy before that version is released.

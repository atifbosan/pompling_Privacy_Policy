Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Drop a pom-pom animal into the basket. When two of the same friend touch, they merge into a bigger one. Keep merging to grow your Pomplings all the way to 2048 — but don't let the basket overflow!

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Pomplings works fully offline.

Are there ads or purchases?
No. The game has no ads and no in-app purchases. Buttons and stars are earned by playing.

Will my progress move to a new phone?
Progress is saved only on your device. It may be included in your device's own iCloud or Google backup, depending on your settings. Uninstalling the game deletes your progress.

How do I reset my progress?
Uninstall and reinstall the game, or clear the app's data in your device settings.

The sound or vibration isn't working.
Check the toggles in the game's Settings, and make sure your device isn't in silent mode.
